## 🔬 Research

### Generative Modeling for Time Series
Diffusion models, generative adversarial networks, and adversarial autoencoders for synthesizing and augmenting time-series data.
> Published at **IEEE ICDM**, **SIAM SDM**, **IEEE BigData**, and **IEEE ICMLA**.

### Multimodal Learning — UNISON
An NSF-funded project (**Award No. 2530946**) extending my generative work across modalities: generating and translating between **images, text, vector data, and time series** through the proposed UNISON framework.

### Space Weather Forecasting
Machine-learning pipelines that forecast **solar flares**, **solar energetic particle (SEP) events**, and **coronal mass ejections (CMEs)**.
> Published in *The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series*, with three manuscripts under review at *Space Weather* and *The Astrophysical Journal*.
